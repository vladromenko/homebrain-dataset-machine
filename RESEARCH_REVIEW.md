# Implementation choices, 27 November 2026

This was a bounded review of primary sources, followed by deployment. Published capabilities are not Homebrain measurements. Exact installed runtime revisions are in `evaluation/environment.json`.

Architecture B is the first deployment: one small shared language server, CPU ASR/VAD/TTS, compact retrieval and cheap continuous image processing. A single multimodal server remains a replaceable configuration, not an assumption about provider capabilities.

## Speech and geometry decisions

[faster-whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper) supplies English recognition and bundled Silero VAD. CPU int8 was chosen to preserve GPU availability. [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) offers a credible ARM/CUDA alternative but compiling a second speech runtime did not help the first usable path. Kokoro is used by the NVIDIA reference and merits a naturalness comparison later; [Piper](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) is the installed lightweight TTS engine. Its GPL license and voice-specific notices are retained. Wake-word and full-duplex behavior remain disabled until false activation and echo are measured.

OpenCV ORB feature matching and RANSAC homography test spatially distributed background agreement. Exposure normalization, match coverage and focus/scale ambiguity prevent ordinary brightness/blur changes being labeled measured camera motion. ArUco detection and PnP support operator-supplied fixed references. Marker corners, intrinsics and resolution must be known before metric pose is valid. Missing references invalidate geometry; uncalibrated image assistance can continue. AprilTag is a viable alternative marker family, not an installed detector in this release.
